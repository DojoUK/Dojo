How we'll use this information

{{ org.name }} collects the details on this form to process your membership application, run your training safely (including any medical information you provide), and — where a guardian is named — to contact them about a junior member. Data is stored for as long as you're an active member and for a limited period afterwards for insurance, safeguarding, and legal record-keeping, then removed or anonymised. It's only visible to {{ org.name }}'s admins and coaches, and to Stripe (payment processing, once you're a paying member) — no one else. You can ask {{ org.name }} to see, correct, or delete what's held about you at any time.
